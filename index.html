1. Does the presentation itself work here

This is the version that needs no network and no student devices. It has to work, so it matters more than anything else on this page.

  1. Open the stage
    Leave it open. This is what goes on the smartboard.
  2. Open the host controls
    In a second tab or window. Press Space. Then switch to the stage tab and watch the clock. If the year and month are moving, everything works.
  3. Back on the host, press 1, then 3, then T.
    1 and 3 pass two policies. T reveals the big red number. If the stage reacts to all three, the whole thing is working on this machine.

2. Can the student machines ever work

Apps Script sends its answers from a second address. If that address is blocked, the nine-Chromebook version is impossible and we stop pursuing it.

  1. script.googleusercontent.com
    A Google error page, or a mostly blank page means the address is allowed. That is what we want.
    The blue Page Blocked screen means it is not, and the nine-machine version is dead.
